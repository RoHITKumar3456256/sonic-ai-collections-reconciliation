# Sonic AI — Architecture & Implementation Explanation

**Project:** Autonomous Collections Agent & Deterministic Bank Reconciliation Platform  
**Target Enterprise:** AcmeCorp India Pvt Ltd  
**Role:** Senior Full-Stack & AI Systems Engineer Assignment Submission  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

In enterprise B2B finance, collections and bank reconciliation are critical operational workflows governed by statutory tax regulations (such as Indian 10% TDS deductions under Form 16A), contractual dispute windows, and banking fee terms.

**Sonic AI** addresses this challenge through an architectural philosophy of **Governed Automation**:
1. **Dynamic Receivables Governance**: Invoices are never statically marked overdue. An invoice is calculated as overdue only when `status == 'sent'`, `balance_due > 0`, and `due_date < reference_date`. Draft and paid invoices are strictly barred from reminder queues.
2. **Context-Isolated LLM Intelligence**: When evaluating customer emails, Gemini receives only the policies and verified invoices of the matched customer. It never sees full customer databases or other accounts.
3. **Ground Truth Fact Verification**: Every invoice number, date, and monetary figure generated by AI is cross-checked against database records before dispatch. Hallucinations or unauthorized commitments immediately trigger human escalation.
4. **The Golden Rule**: A customer's promise or claim of payment **never** marks an invoice paid. Receivables are cleared only through verified bank reconciliation approved by human operators.
5. **Deterministic Bank Statement Matching**: Bank statement rows are matched locally using a 5-signal calibrated algorithm (RapidFuzz string similarity, TDS 10% formula, reference matching, date proximity, and currency constraints). Bank statements are never passed to LLMs.

## 2. Collections Agent & Reply Categorization

The collections agent automates outbound reminder chasing and inbound reply classification across 8 standardized categories:

| Category | Behavior & Escalation | Ground Truth Fact Check |
|---|---|---|
| `routine_query` | Quotes verified open balances and HDFC Bank remittance details. | Verified invoice numbers & balances checked against DB. |
| `payment_promise` | Acknowledges promised date and logs promise in conversation. | Invoice remains strictly unpaid until bank clearance. |
| `payment_claim` | Acknowledges claim / UTR; informs customer that clearance requires bank verification. | Invoice balance is **not** altered. |
| `extension_request` | **Needs Attention Queue**: Due date extensions or installments require human Accounts Team approval. | AI barred from promising date extensions. |
| `dispute` | **Needs Attention Queue**: Assigned to finance operations desk under AcmeCorp 3-day SLA. | Blocks automated chasing. |
| `legal_threat` | **Critical Urgency Queue**: Escalates directly to legal counsel. | Automated outbound communication suppressed. |
| `unknown_sender` | **Needs Attention Queue**: Zero disclosure of invoices or customer data. | Prompts sender for customer credentials. |
| `out_of_scope` | Safe routing acknowledgment or human dispatch. | Prevents off-topic commitments. |

## 3. Bank Statement Reconciliation Engine

Reconciliation processes credit receipts against open invoices.

### Multi-Signal Scoring Heuristics
1. **Invoice Reference (30% weight)**: Detects invoice numbers (e.g. `INV 1004`, `RE 1024`, `INV-1002`) in description strings.
2. **Payer Name Similarity (30% weight)**: Strips legal corporate noise (`PVT LTD`, `CORP`, `INC`, `RTGS`, `DUES`) and calculates token set ratio via RapidFuzz.
3. **Amount Similarity (25% weight)**:
   - **Exact Match**: Receipt equals invoice balance ($\pm 0.50$).
   - **TDS-Adjusted Receipt**: For domestic Indian entities, receipt matches subtotal less 10% TDS plus tax:
     $$\text{Expected Receipt} = (\text{Subtotal} \times 0.90) + \text{Tax}$$
     *Example:* Quillon Tech invoice subtotal ₹300,000 + GST ₹54,000 = Total ₹354,000. Expected receipt with 10% TDS deduction is ₹324,000.
   - **Partial Payment**: Receipt is strictly less than open balance.
4. **Date Proximity (10% weight)**: Compares transaction date against invoice due date.
5. **Currency Match (5% weight)**: Hard check. Currency mismatch immediately zeroes confidence to avoid cross-currency accounting errors.

### Human Approvals & Idempotency
- **Human Choice on Partial / TDS**: The reviewer can choose whether to close the invoice with 10% TDS deduction (upon Form 16A submission) or leave the balance open for partial receipts.
- **Idempotent Approvals**: The system checks if a transaction has already been approved. Duplicate approval requests do not create double payments or alter balances multiple times.
- **Debit Exclusions**: Debit rows (office rent, software vendor) are excluded from receipt matching.

## 4. Technology Stack & Zero-Config Evaluation

- **Frontend**: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS with Lucide icons.
- **Backend**: Python 3.12, FastAPI, Pydantic v2, PyMongo, RapidFuzz.
- **Database Resilience**: Connects to live MongoDB URI if reachable; automatically falls back to high-fidelity in-memory `mongomock` so that evaluators can run the platform immediately without spinning up a local MongoDB server.
- **AI Engine**: Google Gemini API with intelligent fallback rule engine for offline or unauthenticated testing.
- **Email Service**: Outbound SMTP and inbound IMAP with full simulated testing endpoints.
